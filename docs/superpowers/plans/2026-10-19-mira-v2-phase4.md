> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the three placeholder screens with Linear's cycles pages, projects (list, board, timeline), project and initiative pages with updates and health, settings and connect, all in Linear's measured look. The burn-up chart comes last.

**Architecture:**
- **Server:** new reads and writes in `server/app/rally/`:
  - cycle lists and details;
  - a portfolio priority field (D38);
  - comments on projects and initiatives;
  - Lookback burn-up data.
  
  The guard gains the D35 tag rule. Pure rules (cycle stats, burn-up series) live outside the adapter so they test without Rally.
- **Client:**
  - Data comes through `api/client.ts` and new store slices (`store/store.ts`).
  - Pure view logic goes in `pm/model.ts`, `pm/timeline.ts`, `pm/diff.ts`, `pm/health.ts` and `cycles/progress.ts`.
  - Screens are built on shared PM components (`components/pm.tsx`).
  - The issue view gains `cycle` and `project` scopes and ART team scopes.

**Tech stack:** as Phases 1–3 (FastAPI + httpx + pydantic, uv, Python 3.12; React 19, Vite, TS, zustand, Tailwind 4 tokens). No new dependencies. The timeline and burn-up are hand-drawn SVG and CSS.

## Rulings made while planning

Each ruling covers a place where the spec is silent or ambiguous, with what it costs if wrong.

1. **Existing tokens stand in for some §19.2 names.** §19.2's `fill-open` is `pill-on` (lch 94.857), `seg-active` is `tab-on` (95.543), `shadow-popover` is `shadow-menu` (identical value), and `switch-off` already exists. New tokens are created only for the rest (Task 6). *If wrong:* a rename.
2. **Cycle progress:**
   - Scope is the points of every issue in the cycle (no estimate counts 0).
   - Started is In Progress plus In Review.
   - Completed is Done.
   - Percentages are of scope, and 0 scope shows "0%".
   
   *If wrong:* one function, `progressOf`.
3. **Cycle tags:**
   - Current: the cycle contains now.
   - Upcoming: the first cycle starting after now.
   - Planned: later ones.
   - Past cycles get no tag.
   
   For an ART, cycles merge by `cycleKey` (name, start, end), and their stats are summed. *If wrong:* `cycleStatus`.
4. **Timeline zoom scales** (only Year was measured: a 77.5px week): pixels per day are Year 11.07, Quarter 22.14, Month 44.29, Week 110.7. *If wrong:* four constants in `pm/timeline.ts`.
5. **PI names** go through D30's rule in the proxy. "2026 PI4" and "PI4 2026" become "PI 4"; other names pass through. *If wrong:* one regex.
6. **"Show closed projects":**
   - All: everything loaded (open, plus completed in the last 30 days, the existing server window).
   - Recent: completed in the last 14 days.
   - None: hides Completed.
   
   *If wrong:* `pm/model.ts`.
7. **Manual ordering** of projects is Rally's rank: Project gains `rank` from DragAndDropRank. *If wrong:* sort by target instead.
8. **The sparkline** has no history to draw, so it is a straight rising line from (start, 0) to (today, progress). Not drawn at 0%. *If wrong:* replaced by burn-up data later.
9. **Health age** reads "now", "5m", "2h", "3d", "2w", "4mo". Auto health reads "Auto" (D36). *If wrong:* `pm/health.ts`.
10. **The update composer's health** defaults to the latest reported health, else On track. Posting an update waits for Rally, and the button shows "Posting…". Comments on projects and initiatives post optimistically, like issue comments. *If wrong:* the store's `postUpdate`.
11. **Settings › Teams** lists your sidebar and joined teams, plus a "Find a team" search over `GET /api/search` teams. There is no all-teams endpoint, since the workspace has hundreds of projects. *If wrong:* a list endpoint.
12. **Cycle and project issue scopes** load their issues through their own endpoints (`GET /api/teams/:id/cycles/:cycle`, `GET /api/projects/:ref`) into the shared issue cache. The issue view filters the cache by scope.
13. **Project "Teams"** is the feature's own team plus the teams of its issues once its detail has loaded.
14. **The projects Filter's "Dates"** offers "Overdue" and "No target date" only.
15. **Burn-up failures:** Lookback errors and timeouts answer 502. The chart area then shows "Burn-up unavailable" and the legend numbers stay.
16. **D38 values:**
    - Linear Urgent writes the first allowed value of "Urgent" or "Highest".
    - High writes "High".
    - Medium writes "Medium" or "Normal".
    - Low writes "Low".
    - No priority writes empty.
    
    A value the field doesn't allow answers 422. With no field, bootstrap flags are false and the client hides every priority control for projects and initiatives.
17. **Phase 2/3 follow-ups closed here:**
    - Phase 2: the update guard's tag (Task 1); ART team views (Task 7); placeholder wording (removed in Task 8); "Loading Team…" for an unknown team (Task 7).
    - Phase 3 "fix first": all four (Task 19).
    
    The rest stay in the follow-up files.

## Review Focus

These are inputs and failure modes the spec implies but no feature test would naturally exercise. Each has a test in its owning task.

1. **A project with no start, no target, or a start after its target, on the timeline and board.** Bars never get NaN or negative widths. A missing start draws a 1-day bar at the target. No target means no bar, but the row stays in the left panel. *(Task 12.)*
2. **An update or comment refused by the guard** (a real feature, or a `[DEMO]` feature without the tag). The composer keeps the text, a toast says why, and the health in the list and timeline is unchanged. *(Tasks 1, 5 and 14.)*
3. **An ART whose child teams have same-named sprints:**
   - with matching dates they are one cycle row with summed stats;
   - with different dates they stay separate.
   
   Child-team issues appear in the ART's views. *(Tasks 3 and 7.)*
4. **A cycle or project with no estimated issues.** It shows "0%" and empty rings, with no NaN, no division by zero, and an empty sparkline. *(Tasks 8 and 9.)*
5. **A workspace without a portfolio priority field (today's real state).** No Priority column, property, filter, picker or grouping appears anywhere, and a saved view that had Priority falls back cleanly. *(Tasks 2, 10 and 15.)*
