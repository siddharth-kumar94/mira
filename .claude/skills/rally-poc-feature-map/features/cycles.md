# Sprints

Last verified: f064d7ee53a4 on 2026-10-20 (live)

A team's sprints (Rally Iterations), in Rally's words: the Sprints list (`/team/<id>/cycles`) with a rail of dates, and the sprint page (`/team/<id>/cycles/<id>`) with the story view and a side panel of progress and breakdowns. Everyone uses it. It lives in `web/src/cycles/` over `server/app/cycles.py` and `server/app/rally/issues.py`. Overall: beta. The numbers match Rally and the screens, burn-up included, were driven live, but an ART was never available to drive, the burn-up's projection corners are sharp where Linear's are rounded, and several details were guessed rather than measured.

## How to get to it (user POV)

1. In the sidebar, under a team, open "Sprints".
2. The current sprint is expanded. Click a row to open that sprint's page.
3. On the page, the right panel shows Progress and the breakdown tabs "Assignees", "Labels", "Priority", "Features". The toolbar's last button hides the panel.
4. The "PI 4 · Sprint 3 ⌄" switcher in the breadcrumb jumps to another sprint.

## Driving it

Preconditions: baseline.

- **cycles-stats**, **cycles-names**: → `rallypoc check api` → `api:cycles-read` (9 sprints, current PI 4 · Sprint 3), `api:cycle-detail-read`, `api:cycle-names` all PASS.
- **cycles-list**, **cycles-page**, **cycles-panel**, **cycles-breakdown**: → `rallypoc browser run cycles` → PASS: a Current row opens the sprint page and every breakdown tab shows rows.
- **cycles-art**: → `rallypoc check api` → `api:cycle-detail-art` SKIP ("no ART in your teams"). Join an ART in Settings › Teams to drive it.
- **cycles-burnup**: → `rallypoc browser run cycles` → PASS: the current row and the sprint panel each show the chart (or "Burn-up unavailable"). → `rallypoc api GET "/api/burnup?team=<id>&cycle=<current>"` → 200, and the last point's scope, started and completed equal the sprint's stats from `GET /api/teams/<id>/cycles`.

## Gotchas

- **The burn-up lags Rally by minutes after a write.** Lookback is eventually consistent: during `check ui` on 2026-10-20 the demo sprint's legend (WSAPI) read started 6 while the burn-up's last point still read 11, and right after `rallypoc seed` the reverse; a minute or two later both read 18 / 11 / 2. The legend is the live number; the chart catches up.
- **The sprints list reads fast now** (0.9 s on 2026-10-20); it was 17 to 37 s before the sprint reads stopped matching every team's same-named iteration (`3301a0f`).
- **Same-named sprints merge only in ART views, and only when their dates match** (spec §16 #8). My stories never merges them.
- **Next year's iterations are already there.** AI/ML Solutions lists a Planned "PI 1 · Sprint 1" at the top, above the current PI's rows, because Rally already holds iterations for the PI after next.
